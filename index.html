The Odyssey, on the hive

One story, three tellings: Homer's poem, EPIC: The Musical, and Christopher Nolan's 2026 film. Each node type lives on its own axis, and every curve is a relationship from a Neo4j graph, drawn with d3. The gold chain down the Places axis is Odysseus's voyage from Troy home to Ithaca. Hover anything for more, and turn on song previews to hear the musical.
